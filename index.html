IInterior design

Rooms that feel as if they have always been there

We begin with how you live, then shape every room around it. Natural materials, quiet colour and furniture made to last are layered with the pieces you already love, so the house feels settled from the day you move in.

  • Whole-home interior design
  • Joinery and furniture design
  • Colour, material and lighting schemes
  • Furniture, art and antique sourcing

IICuration and personal touches

Your story, kept in the details

An inherited armchair, tiles carried home from a holiday, the key to a grandmother's house. We gather what matters to you and give it a place, beside materials that age gracefully: unlacquered brass, honed marble, oiled oak and linen.

  • Material and finish selection
  • Heirloom and artwork placement
  • Upholstery and soft furnishings
  • Styling for the final layer

IIIConstruction consulting

From the first sketch to the final inspection

Renovating is a long road with many hands. We help you plan it properly, then stay beside you through approvals, pricing and the build, so the finished house matches the one you imagined.

  • Feasibility, budgets and staging
  • Drawings and documentation for approval
  • Builder selection and quote review
  • Site visits and quality checks

IVHeritage conservation

Caring for the beauty of the past

Old houses carry the skill of the people who built them. We help owners of heritage-listed and period homes restore what matters and adapt the rest with sympathy, guided by the Burra Charter's principle of changing as much as necessary but as little as possible.

  • Heritage advice and conservation strategy
  • Sympathetic alterations and additions
  • Joinery, plasterwork, tiles and iron lace
  • Historic colour schemes

VThe process

How we work together

  1. I

    Conversation

    We meet at your home, listen to how you live and look closely at the building itself.

  2. II

    Concept

    Layouts, colour, materials and furniture come together in a scheme you can see and touch.

  3. III

    Documentation

    Drawings, specifications and approvals, prepared so builders can price and build with confidence.

  4. IV

    Construction

    We help you choose the builder, then visit site to keep quality, budget and detail on track.

  5. V

    Living

    Furniture, art and the final styling, placed so you can move straight in and stay for years.

VIEnquiries

Let's begin with a conversation

Tell us about your home, what you love about it and what you would change. We take on a small number of projects at a time, so each one has our full attention.
